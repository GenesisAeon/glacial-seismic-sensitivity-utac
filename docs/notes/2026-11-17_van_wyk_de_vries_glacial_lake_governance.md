## Relevance for P133

- P133 tests whether earthquake catalogues near melting glaciers show an early change
  in sensitivity to seasonal unloading. This comment does not supply data, a method, or
  a calibration target for those tests.
- It is relevant only as context for hazard cascades that accompany deglaciation:
  expanding glacial lakes behind unstable natural dams, downstream consequences, and a
  call for consequence-based, risk-tiered monitoring analogous to engineered reservoirs,
  including clearer protection during conflict.
- It should not be read as evidence for or against glacial-unloading seismicity.
